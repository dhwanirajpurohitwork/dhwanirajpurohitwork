### Featured Build: CIRCLIQ
**AI & Blockchain-Based Circular Lifecycle Intelligence Platform**

Most fashion tech stops at tracking a garment's origin. **CIRCLIQ** answers the next critical question: *Given a garment's real-time condition and market economics, what is the highest-value next step - Repair, Resell, or Recycle?*

* **Computer Vision Condition Scoring:** Evaluates uploaded garment images to detect wear and assign an objective condition score.
* **ML Resale Price Predictor:** Regression pipeline estimating secondary-market value based on brand, material, age, and condition.
* **Circular Decision Engine:** Calculates net economic recovery (Resale Value vs. Repair & Logistics Costs) to automate lifecycle routing.
* **Digital Product Passports & Trust Layer:** QR-linked lifecycle history backed by immutable on-chain verification events.

**Architecture & Stack:** `Python` • `Scikit-learn` • `OpenCV` • `FastAPI` • `React` • `Node.js` • `MongoDB` • `Solidity (Polygon)`
